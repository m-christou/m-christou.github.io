Description
======

Controversial concepts and theories heavily inform medical practice. Sometimes, debates in medicine are at their core about the conceptual apparatus that informs its practice. For this reason, it is essential to have a firm grasp of the philosophical dimension underlying medical concepts and practice. This survey course is an introduction to the philosophical debates around the nature of modern medicine. Topics included in this survey are the concepts of health and disease, the nature of evidence and inferences in medicine, and ethical and epistemological issues in public health.  

**Semesters taught**: Winter Semester 2024, 2025, 2026

Topics
======

Theories of Health and Disease, Causality in Medicine, Evidence-Based Medicine, Value Judgments, Objectivity in Medicine
